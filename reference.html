My pricing formula

My floor cost = materials per piece + (my hourly rate × hours per piece) + overhead per piece
My launch price = floor × 1.30 (minimum) to floor × 1.50 (maximum)
My progressive increases = +10–15% on the current price after every 6–10 units sold — prints and originals only
I never price anything below my floor. I track sales counts per piece or edition to apply increases on schedule.

How I apply this
I choose one band size and stick to it: every 6 units (faster increases, suited to my originals with limited quantity) or every 10 units (gentler curve, suited to my print editions up to 100).

I compound on my current price, not my launch price. Each increase applies to whatever the price is now, not where it started. I round to the nearest $5 for clean listings.

I announce it in my shop. Stating "price increases after every 10 sold" gives buyers a genuine reason to act — the structure does the work without me having to pressure anyone.

For my originals: since each is one of a kind, I apply this rule per series or per surface type within a body of work. I track how many originals of a given type have sold and raise accordingly on future pieces of the same type.

My channel adjustments

Direct (Shopify / my website): I use my prices as listed above.
Gallery or shop (40–50% commission): I gross up my price so my net is always protected.
My gallery list price = my target net ÷ (1 − commission rate)
Example: I want to net $300 → I list at $545 for a 45% commission gallery. My progressive increases still apply to the gallery list price.
I keep my direct and gallery list prices publicly consistent. If a gallery lists a piece at $545, I match that price on my website — or I negotiate exclusivity terms with the gallery.

My member discount (when I'm ready to launch it)

I plan to offer 10% off prints and originals for active postcard club members, applied at checkout via discount code. The discount applies to my current band price — not my original launch price. My progressive increases still apply; members simply pay 10% less than my public price at each band.

All prices in CAD. My floors use midpoint estimates: $375 materials, $62/hr labour, $500+ studio overhead ÷ 6 pieces/month.

My launch markup is 30–50% above floor. My progressive increases compound on the current price at each band; I round to the nearest $5.

My $5 finishing cost per print is included in my Tier 2 floor costs. Finishing for originals (box, corner protectors, foam) to be calculated separately.

I refine my floor costs as I track my actual hours per piece and actual materials spend each month.
